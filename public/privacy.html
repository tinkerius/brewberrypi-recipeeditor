Privacy policy

BrewberryPi Recipe Editor, https://recipes.brewberrypi.org — last changed 6 November 2026

In short

The recipe editor runs entirely in your browser. There is no BrewberryPi server behind it: your recipes and your Google account data never pass through or are stored by BrewberryPi.

Without Cloud Sync

Recipes you create or open are stored only in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device, so they are still there after a reload. They are not sent anywhere. Files you download are saved by your browser to your device. Clearing your browser data deletes the stored recipes.

Cloud Sync (Google Drive)

When you sign in with Google, the editor asks Google for access to the files it creates or that you open with it in your Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in your Drive. With this access, and only to show and save your recipes, your browser talks directly to Google:

  • it creates the folder BrewberryPi with the subfolder recipes in your Drive;
  • it lists, reads, writes, renames and moves to the trash the recipe files in that folder;
  • it reads your name and e-mail address to show which account is signed in.

The access token Google issues expires after about an hour. Until then, or until you sign out, it is kept in your browser's local storage on this device, so other tabs of the editor and reloads stay signed in; signing out in one tab signs out all of them and removes it. To make the next sign-in quicker, the editor also remembers your e-mail address there until you sign out. Nothing else is stored, and no data from Google is shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The editor's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the editor's access at any time in your Google account under Third-party apps & services. Your recipe files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

The BrewberryPi app's Cloud Sync

If you turn on Cloud Sync in the BrewberryPi app, your device signs in to your Google account itself (you confirm it with a code on another device), with the same permission: only the files of the recipe editor. It only reads the folder BrewberryPi/recipes and copies those recipes to the device; it never writes to your Google Drive. The device keeps its Google sign-in (and your e-mail address, to show the account) in the app's configuration folder on the device, which the device's file share also shows; disconnecting Cloud Sync in the app deletes it. Nothing of this goes to BrewberryPi.

Hosting

The pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver them; see the GitHub privacy statement. The editor uses no cookies of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Google's sign-in and file picker are loaded from Google when you use them.
